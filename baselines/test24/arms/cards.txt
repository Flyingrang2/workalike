Rules the person at this desk confirmed from their own work. Follow them for the choices they cover. If a choice comes up that no rule here covers, or information you need is missing, do not guess: ask the owner with ask_owner.
R1. When Requested SKU/quantity not fully in stock and/or restock is after the requested ship date, customer does not insist on exact item/date: Inform the customer of on-hand quantity and restock date; propose options such as shipping available quantity now and remainder after restock, waiting for full restock, or substituting with an in-stock item. Do not quote the original SKU for the requested ship date if restock is after that date. May create a mixed original+substitute quote and an all-substitute alternative. If a substitute is offered because of a stockout, offer an apology discount within worker discount authority and seek owner approval when the worker has no authority.
R2. When Requested SKU/quantity cannot be fulfilled by the requested ship date and the customer explicitly insists the item must be exact and the date must be met with no substitutes: Inform the customer of the shortage and inability to meet the date, apologize, and close without offering a substitute, partial shipment, wait option or a quote.
R3. When Customer provides an SKU that is not found in the catalog: Check stock for the unknown SKU, receive an unknown_sku error with similar suggestions, check stock for the suggested similar SKU(s), and ask the customer to confirm the intended SKU before proceeding with pricing or quoting.
R4. When Customer request does not include an explicit ship-by date: Ask the customer for an acceptable ship date before checking costs, calculating price or creating a quote; may tell the customer the earliest date the shop could ship.
R5. When Customer provides a latest acceptable ship date for a new or reorder order: Set the quote ship_by date on or before the requested latest date, never later. Typically set 1-2 days earlier than the customer's stated latest, often 1 day earlier for new quotes and 2 days earlier for repeat quotes that reuse an approved price, and often move off weekends.
R6. When Customer asks to reorder the same quantities as a previous approved quote that is still within its valid_until date: Look up the previous quote, verify it is approved and valid_until is on or after today, and reuse the previous unit price instead of recalculating price from current cost. Note that the price is from the approved quote.
R7. When Customer requests a reorder referencing a previous quote that is expired: Look up the previous quote, note its expiration, check current stock and cost, create a new quote at today's list/current prices and explicitly inform the customer the previous quote has expired so the new quote is at today's prices. May offer a reorder discount up to worker authority after a price complaint.
R8. When Customer questions a price increase on a re-quote or explicitly requests a lower price/discount: If the discount is within the worker's stated discount authority and at or above the floor price, apply it directly and note the discount % as within authority. If the discount exceeds authority or would go below floor price, ask the owner for permission before offering a reduced price and only proceed if the owner agrees to stay at or above floor. Never quote below floor price. Discounts may be framed as a reorder discount.
R9. When Before quoting any line item: Always verify stock sufficiency for the requested qty/sku with check_stock before get_cost/calc_price/create_quote.
R10. When Customer follows up on a request that was previously acknowledged but not yet quoted: Apologize for the wait referencing the prior acknowledgement date in the reply, then provide the quote.
